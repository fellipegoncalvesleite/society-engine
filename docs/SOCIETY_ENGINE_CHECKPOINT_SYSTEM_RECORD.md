## 0.1 Status vocabulary

- **CLOSED / ACCEPTED / FROZEN** — independently accepted checkpoint with a published authority.
- **ACTIVE / OPEN** — roadmap implementation/correction currently in progress and not frozen.
- **REQUIRED ARCHITECTURE GATE** — mandatory research/design/user-decision boundary; not an ordinary
  simulation feature and not permission to implement the next item.
- **CLOSED / RESOLVED ARCHITECTURE GATE** — the gate produced an accepted architectural/roadmap
  decision; the gate itself remains non-simulation authority.
- **ACTIVE FOUNDATION PROGRAM** — a multi-checkpoint inserted foundation whose completed sub-checkpoints
  may be frozen while later sub-checkpoints remain open/future.
- **FUTURE** — roadmap item not opened, even when predecessor code has similar names.
- **LEGACY / PREDECESSOR** — published code relevant to a future item but outside that item's accepted
  canonical solution.
- **PROJECTION ONLY** — derives/displays information but does not change simulation behavior.
- **INSPECTED LOCAL SOURCE / UNPUSHED** — exact local source inspected but not accepted published
  authority.
- **TYPE-ONLY SCAFFOLD** — schema without production collection/writer/lifecycle.
- **DORMANT / DEAD VOCABULARY** — names with no meaningful producer/reader lifecycle.
- **DOCUMENTATION DRIFT** — prose/metadata that disagrees with accepted Git/evidence.

# 2. Roadmap Item 1 — Ordinary Exploration Capacity

**Status:** CLOSED / ACCEPTED / FROZEN  
**Published authority:** `checkpoint/ordinary-exploration-capacity-24` at
`ce723b3f1973e4f3f2c54a424a614e723a14558a`  
**Primary evidence:** `docs/evidence/correction24c/`

## 2.1 Human explanation

A band can send people beyond the country it already knows without being told a hidden rich
destination. The party follows a direction justified by the band's own remembered corridors,
visible relief or water margins, inherited directional hints, known range edges, or bounded
second-hand information. It walks one physical step at a time, retains a bounded trail, keeps enough
reserve to retrace that trail, and eventually returns or fails.

Only after the party physically returns does the band acquire the observations it carried. Those
returned observations become canonical `KnownTileRecord`s. They are not decorative: later production
readers consult them when evaluating movement destinations, camp movement, resource activity, range
saturation and, rarely, daughter-fission targets. A changed exploration record can therefore alter a
later selected action, a physical trip or movement, and a food receipt.

Exploration does not mean that discovering more country automatically helps. Long-horizon evidence
found interaction-dependent effects, including cases where more exploration cost worker-days and
reduced receipts. The checkpoint closes truthful capacity and causal use, not a promise that
exploration is always beneficial.

## 2.2 Owned causal chain

```text
band-known pressure or opportunity
→ frontier-exploration eligibility
→ band-known directional hypothesis, not an unseen destination
→ physically contiguous outbound steps
→ bounded reserve and retrace requirement
→ observations held by the party
→ physical return
→ canonical observed-tile write
→ actual production reader consultation
→ possible decision/output/action/receipt divergence
```

## 2.3 Canonical code and authority

- `src/sim/agents/expedition.ts` owns the multi-day party lifecycle and the real frontier-return write.
- `src/sim/agents/frontierExploration.ts` owns direction-based frontier planning and step selection.
- `KnownTileRecord` in band knowledge is the returned information authority.
- Actual readers were instrumented at their production call sites, not inferred from state hashes:
  movement/carrying/range readers, camp movement, resource activity, and fission-target selection.
- Residential movement, camp movement, resource activity and daughter creation were recorded as
  separate physical-action identities; candidate arrays were not counted as actions.
- Route/corridor memory is **not** a reader of an exploration-returned tile record. Corridors are
  written from executed residential movement. This structural no-reader is intentional and should
  not be "fixed" by inventing a route effect.

## 2.4 Evidence that matters

Published evidence reports:

- 686 admitted counterfactual replay events across 40, 200 and 500-year horizons;
- 686 sound controls and zero unsound controls;
- every retained sampled record reached an actual production reader;
- 18 physical-action divergences at 40 years, 40 at 200 years, and 4 at 500 years;
- receipt/support divergences at all three horizons;
- zero direct demographic divergences in this particular record-suppression experiment;
- 12/12 controlled fixtures, zero failing and zero vacuous;
- a corrected structural proof that a retained record is read by `getKnownTileStats` no later than
  the next seasonal decision: 17,554 full follow windows, zero never consulted, maximum measured
  first-read delay 88 days against a 90-day season;
- exact same-seed replay, step-mode invariance and bounded knowledge/history stores.

## 2.5 Permanent invariants

Future work must preserve:

1. an exploration party cannot learn from a place before physical or otherwise explicitly justified
   observation returns;
2. a band cannot choose an unseen rich tile from world truth;
3. the route is contiguous and bounded;
4. need may change willingness but not manufacture pace, provisions, knowledge or carrying capacity;
5. returned knowledge may be consequential without being guaranteed beneficial;
6. actual readers and physical actions remain distinguishable from candidate scores and diagnostic
   projections;
7. daughter knowledge inheritance remains partial and degraded.

## 2.6 Accepted simplifications

- Parties are aggregate groups, not named individuals or households.
- The directional hypothesis is a bounded abstraction, not a culturally transmitted migration plan.
- Trip-local provisions are accounting abstractions rather than withdrawals from a complete household
  inventory.
- The checkpoint does not model teaching, elder carriers, family composition or permanent migration
  institutions.

## 2.7 Known weaknesses and future audit targets

- Natural fission consultation of returned exploration records was scarce.
- The counterfactual demonstrated causal use but no universal population or fitness improvement.
- The expedition and movement substrate is large legacy code; Item 13 must audit whether all
  residential journeys and crossings still share one mobility authority.
- Item 14 must replace aggregate "party workers" with a reusable task-group authority without
  reopening physical route and return invariants.
- Item 21 may replace aggregate knowledge transfer with real carriers, but must preserve acquisition,
  degradation and loss.
- Performance rises with band count and long horizons; later performance work must not change
  epistemic or physical semantics silently.

## 2.8 Reopening triggers

Reopen Item 1 only if evidence shows teleportation, hidden-truth targeting, knowledge before return,
missing/duplicated bodies, an unbounded route or store, two competing frontier writers, or a future
carrier model that cannot express the frozen return-to-knowledge contract.

# 3. Roadmap Item 2 — Resource Investigation / Temporary Use

**Status:** CLOSED / ACCEPTED / FROZEN  
**Published authority:** `checkpoint/resource-investigation-physical-26` at
`5f341648addcd4331e86d340f21d2a830b703095`  
**Diagnostic predecessor:** `checkpoint/resource-investigation-authority-25` at
`f94755047b2ea2a47b9453f3917e8eaf67816ca3`  
**Primary evidence:** `docs/evidence/resource-investigation-authority-25/` and
`docs/evidence/resource-investigation-physical-26/`

## 3.1 Human explanation

A band may decide that a known or suspected patch deserves investigation without moving the whole
residential group. The old seasonal action looked physical but was not: it could revise knowledge
about a target up to ten tiles away while no worker left, no route was crossed, no day passed and no
failure was possible.

The closed checkpoint keeps the seasonal decision but turns it into a bounded pending intention. On
the next eligible daily-action day, the existing physical trip machinery attempts the investigation.
The group needs actual workers and a contiguous passable route. If it arrives, it observes from the
place where it actually stands and then returns. If it cannot go, the attempt receives a named
outcome instead of silently updating knowledge.

This means investigation can be selected but not executed. It may be beyond same-day reach, have no
route, lack labor, become invalid, be superseded, expire, or be cancelled because the band moved or
ceased to be active. No observation occurs merely because the idea won a seasonal score.

## 3.2 Owned causal chain

```text
band-known uncertainty / value of information
→ seasonal scout or logistical-probe decision
→ exact pending investigation identity, capped at one per band
→ no observation at selection
→ next eligible daily action
→ real worker allocation and route feasibility
→ physical arrival or named non-execution
→ canonical observation only at arrival
→ memory update and later behavioral use
→ bounded terminal outcome
```

## 3.3 Canonical code and authority

- `src/sim/agents/pendingInvestigation.ts` owns the bounded pending record and outcomes.
- `src/sim/agents/intraSeasonTrips.ts` owns execution through the sanctioned daily-action phase.
- `src/sim/agents/resourceScoutObservation.ts` owns the observation-domain logic moved out of the
  seasonal applier.
- `src/sim/rules/bandDecision.ts` continues to select the seasonal action but no longer grants free
  knowledge at selection.
- `campMovement.ts`, event/story projections and UI were corrected so a selected probe is not
  narrated as a physical temporary camp. A real same-day party record is written only after actual
  departure; genuine multi-day `ExpeditionTaskCamp` remains separate.

## 3.4 Physical range boundary

The legacy candidate search and daily trip candidate radius are both ten tiles, but the existing
same-day round-trip budget is eight tiles. Under the current production formula, only one-way
distance four or less is honestly same-day. The checkpoint therefore refuses farther attempts as
`beyond_same_day_reach` instead of compressing them into a fake day.

This closes truthfulness under the current mobility boundary. It does **not** prove that the boundary
itself is scientifically correct. Item 13 may later refine mobility, but it must do so by changing the
canonical mobility authority and rerunning these invariants.

## 3.5 Evidence that matters

Published evidence reports:

- before correction: 192 selected investigations and 176 target-area knowledge gains at selection;
- after correction: 234 selected, zero gains at selection, 97 later physical executions, 132 named
  non-executions and 5 still pending in that measurement window;
- broader natural sample: 343 selected, 139 executed and returned, 147 beyond same-day reach, 54 no
  route, 3 still pending, zero silently lost;
- information-only investigations structurally cannot deposit food receipts or support;
- successful physical arrival may teach more strongly than the fake old observation because the
  party truly stands at the target; this is explicit rather than disguised;
- a step-mode timestamp regression was found and fixed, and the fixture was strengthened with a
  negative control that fails when the bug is restored.

## 3.6 Permanent invariants

Future work must preserve:

1. selection is not execution;
2. an information attempt has an exact identity and bounded resolution;
3. observation requires actual arrival or another explicitly modeled acquisition path;
4. no information-only action produces calories or a food receipt;
5. workers, route, duration and failure are physical rather than score-only costs;
6. a same-day party is not a camp, and a multi-day expedition camp is a different authority;
7. daily/seasonal step modes produce equivalent timestamps and outcomes.

## 3.7 Accepted simplifications and debts

- Over-range investigation is a named refusal rather than transfer to a multi-day expedition, because
  no existing expedition family truthfully represented the exact question.
- `temporary_use` remained dormant because its existing timing makes its answer arrive too late for
  the camp decision it would inform.
- `route_unavailable` does not yet create bounded negative target memory, so repeated reselection is a
  known risk.
- Dynamic mobility versus the fixed same-day budget was not tested.
- An inherited expedition-lifecycle audit failure was recorded unchanged rather than hidden.

## 3.8 Future extension points

- Item 13 may create an honest multi-day investigation transfer and dynamic mobility classification.
- Item 14 may turn aggregate workers into reusable task groups.
- Item 15 may make temporary use leave physical traces or sites.
- Item 16 may add tools, containers and task-specific material requirements.
- Item 21 may add actual knowledge carriers and teaching after return.

None of these may restore knowledge at selection, free remote observation or information-as-food.

# 4. Roadmap Item 3 — Crowding / Shared Range / Range Release

**Status:** CLOSED / ACCEPTED / FROZEN  
**Accepted production authority:** `706166892d40189fc56ac7458b9e90a8ffdbddd7`  
**Final certification:** `checkpoint/shared-range-item-3-final-freeze-36` at
`ef76971bd66a7413313183349b9468a879405970`  
**Primary evidence:** `docs/evidence/shared-range-item-3-final-freeze-36/`

## 4.1 Human explanation

A band can be affected by other people only through distinct causal layers. Bodies first have to be
somewhere. Current physical proximity creates crowding and can create an encounter opportunity. An
attributable encounter may create friction evidence. The observing band stores that episode and can
form an expectation about using a particular place. That expectation may influence movement, but it
cools over time. When all contributing evidence reaches zero weight, the expectation becomes
historical: the record remains inspectable but changes no behavior. A returning band does not
magically reactivate old released evidence; a new episode must earn new weight.

The checkpoint also repaired away-party accounting. People on expeditions are represented at their
physical position rather than at home, productive labor is distinguished from non-working party
members, distant work must be performed by positive workers at the target, and food supports the
residential band only after cargo physically returns.

## 4.2 Owned causal chain

```text
physical co-presence
→ crowding
→ encounter opportunity
→ attributable friction
→ remembered access expectation from held records
→ active / cooling / released-historical lifecycle
→ decision-facing movement pressure
→ residential extraction and away-party presence
→ productive target work
→ physical cargo and return
→ historical persistence without current action
```

## 4.3 Canonical authority map

- **Physical presence:** `crowding.getBandPhysicalPresence`; residential remainder at home plus each
  away party at its own current tile.
- **Crowding:** `crowdingPenalty` from present physical proximity only.
- **Encounter admission:** `socialContext.getEncounterCandidatePairs` and `getEncounterKind`, bounded
  by current proximity.
- **Friction:** `RangeFrictionEvent` requiring the other band in the observer's current proximity set.
- **Access expectation:** `accessNorms.deriveAccessMemory`, recomputed from the band's own held records,
  not from a world census.
- **Release:** evidence weight and explicit active/cooling/released-historical phases; zero weight means
  zero current behavior while records remain.
- **Decision pressure:** `pressureState.mobilityPressure` and downstream candidate scoring.
- **Residential extraction:** `sharedCatchment.getBandForagingFootprint`, currently anchored on the
  residential catchment.
- **Away bodies and work:** expedition workers plus non-working party people; target work requires a
  positive productive-worker authority.
- **Food:** physical harvest to cargo to return receipt to human support.

## 4.4 Evidence that matters

Published freeze evidence reports:

- 16/16 integrated fixtures, zero failing, zero vacuous and zero not-constructed in that integrated
  set;
- natural 20, 50 and 200-year runs plus a second 200-year seed with zero adverse conservation or
  lifecycle contradictions;
- 627,479 band-days with zero presence-sum mismatch, ghost body-day, missing body or conservation
  failure;
- hundreds of released expectations at 200 years and zero still affecting behavior;
- deterministic lifecycle trajectories and flat reported state/performance bounds across 20/50/200
  years;
- all 607 previously accepted evidence files hash-identical before and after the freeze regression.

The checkpoint claims truthfulness and causal separation. It explicitly claims no survival, fitness
or outcome improvement.

## 4.5 Territorial vocabulary is not territoriality

Three suggestive names exist without a lived territorial authority:

- `Band.territorialPressure` is retained but behaviorally inert after readers were removed;
- `SocialPressureProfile.territorialPressure` has zero readers;
- `Reason<"territorial_pressure">` has zero producers.

A future Item 27 territoriality/access system may claim or replace these names only with a new lived
writer, explicit readers and migration evidence. Merely reconnecting the old numbers is forbidden.

## 4.6 Permanent invariants

Future work must preserve:

1. physical presence is not memory;
2. crowding is current physical proximity, not a remembered place;
3. encounter, recognition, friction, access expectation, hostility and territorial claim remain
   separate;
4. a band acts only on evidence it holds;
5. released historical evidence contributes exactly zero current behavior while history remains;
6. away bodies cannot forage at home and work away simultaneously;
7. physical people, productive labor, stock, cargo and returned support remain separately accounted;
8. future access norms or territory cannot be inferred directly from overlap.

## 4.7 Six carried-forward seams

These remain open and must not be mistaken for Item 3 behavior:

1. the shared-use footprint is residence-anchored; trips, expeditions and investigation walks do not
   yet create physical competition along their activity range;
2. activity-party crowding and expedition/task-party overlap are absent;
3. social perception has no visibility, route or terrain-barrier rule;
4. there is no physical-trace authority for tracks, trails, camp remains, smoke or freshness;
5. the second territorial-pressure field is an inert orphan;
6. no UI currently exposes the full social-evidence cooling/release lifecycle.

## 4.8 Accepted abstractions and suspicious results

- Aggregate bands and parties stand in for individuals and households.
- Proximity is terrain-blind detection.
- Provisions are trip-local accounting, not a fully conserved residential store.
- Cooling is time-based rather than season-aware.
- Reports preserve hops and freshness but not a social graph.
- Crowding was not shown to flip a natural action by itself, and its coefficient is an authority rather
  than an empirical calibration.
- Some threshold and concurrent-party fixtures required explicit non-vacuity handling; these incidents
  are preserved in the freeze evidence.

## 4.9 Future extension points

- Item 13 owns activity-range movement, crossings and same-day overlap.
- Item 14 owns reusable task-party composition and coordination.
- Item 15 owns persistent traces and sites.
- Item 23 owns richer relationships and reputation.
- Item 27 owns hospitality, tenure, claims and territoriality.
- Item 41 owns public historical explanation of the lifecycle.

These items may enrich the social meaning of shared range but may not rewrite who was physically
present or make memories into crowding.

# 5. Roadmap Item 4 — Dynamic Fission / Daughter Viability / Successor Groups

**Status:** CLOSED / ACCEPTED / FROZEN  
**Accepted base:** `b661d41393ec95e0d119d9e0c9855382e3b0fb7e`  
**Certified production/audit code:** `717ffd908c6e0f993670e2343d0b91bca74b383c`  
**Final freeze/certification authority:** `11f085930462f981fcca45faf37b67d961196adb`  
**Primary frozen evidence:** `docs/evidence/item4-final-freeze/`

## 5.1 Human explanation

Fission is no longer an annual teleport that instantly manufactures an ordinary daughter band.
Ordinary simulation can now create a proposed split, plan and prepare it, require a real founder
commitment and one-use authorization, move exact cohorts out of the parent, create a physically real
but provisional successor at the parent's origin, and make that group travel, feed itself, attempt
independent operation, fail, return, reintegrate, die, or eventually establish.

A departure is not success. A provisional successor is a real group of bodies but remains quarantined
from established-only behavior until the lifecycle proves an honest terminal outcome. Successful
stabilization requires real post-departure independent operation. A failed return remains a distinct
historical course and can end in reintegration, extinction, unresolved physical failure followed by a
fresh survivor commitment, or `established_after_failed_return` under fresh evidence.

This checkpoint closes the foundational physical truth of creating a successor group. Later kinship,
households, culture, leadership, genetics and membership systems may enrich who leaves, why they
leave and what the split means socially. They must not recreate teleportation, duplicate bodies,
automatic success or a second daughter authority.

## 5.2 Owned causal chain

```text
ecological/demographic pressure
→ natural fission proposal
→ bounded proposal lifecycle
→ target/planning
→ exact founder allocation
→ residual-parent viability ruling
→ founder commitment
→ one-use departure authorization
→ canonical preparation / freshness
→ departure_ready
→ daily physical departure after the ready day
→ atomic cohort transfer
→ provisional successor at physical origin
→ contiguous travel
→ real provisional subsistence / depletion / embodied condition
→ establishing
→ either positive independent operation and stabilization
   OR failed_early → returning → physical reintegration
   OR failed return → unresolved_after_failed_return
      → fresh current-survivor commitment / new course
      → established_after_failed_return, reintegration or extinction
→ bounded lifecycle/history projection
→ physical-separation cooldown for any later fission
```

Parent attempt deadlines and provisional lifecycle deadlines bound unresolved states. Zero physical
population resolves through the provisional lifecycle rather than leaving a ghost group.

## 5.3 Canonical authority map

- `bandLifecycle.ts` answers semantic questions such as terminal, living, provisional, established,
  fission-eligible, in-transit and current parent/successor lineage relation.
- `fissionFounderAllocation.ts` allocates exact founder cohorts from actual parent cohorts.
- `fissionParentResidualViability.ts` separates pre-existing hardship from damage caused by the split.
- founder commitment and departure authorization preserve the accepted cohort/destination and permit
  exactly one physical departure.
- `prepareFissionDeparture` owns canonical pre-departure preparation/freshness.
- `performAtomicDeparture` in `fissionDepartureSeam.ts` is the sole body-transfer authority.

health/provisioning/life-course prerequisites. Carry DIAG-1's eventual findings into this chapter
without rewriting the old scenario as if it had been reproduced already.

### Motivating observation — hypothesis, not conclusion

The user reported a diagnostic long-run case in which a band occupying a location perceived from the
outside as **very rich** experienced hunger for roughly **1,850 of 2,000 simulated years** and roughly
**60% average extinction risk**. The broader user-observed pattern was:

- bad environments: populations decline/die;
- average environments: populations decline/die;
- the most favorable observed environment: survival/stabilization appears possible, but sustained
  positive population growth appears weak relative to the user's intuition for a small group in an
  exceptional habitat.

This is **not yet a proven simulator defect**. The user's classification comes from outside the causal
model and may reflect a map/UI projection, habitat potential or another proxy rather than abundant
usable human support. The observation is a named audit hypothesis that must be reproduced, measured
and causally decomposed before any calibration change.

Name the future diagnostic scenario:

**Rich-Environment Survival Pathology Regression**

Its first job is to recover the exact map/seed/location/version if possible and establish what made the
place appear rich, what physical resources actually existed, what the band knew/reached/harvested,
how hunger-years were counted, and how the ~60% mean extinction-risk statistic was derived.

### Core research question

> Across empirically plausible ecological and survival regimes, does Society Engine produce realistic
> relationships between environmental quality, realized human support, population density, fertility,
> mortality and long-run population growth?

A second load-bearing question is:

> Does a place that looks exceptional in a projection actually become an exceptional human
> environment after physical source availability, knowledge, access, labor, range, harvest and
> provisioning are accounted for?

### Required causal decomposition

Instrument the complete chain:

```text
environmental productivity
→ materialized physical plant/fauna/aquatic/water opportunity
→ band knowledge and uncertainty
→ accessibility / range / labor / logistics
→ realized physical harvest
→ returned food receipts
→ food per person and provisioning support
→ seasonal + chronic hunger classification
→ health/mortality pressure
→ fertility response
→ births
→ infant/child/dependent mortality where represented
→ adult mortality
→ elder mortality
→ cohort/dependency structure
→ births - deaths = net growth
→ density / crowding / depletion feedback
→ long-run growth, equilibrium, migration, crash, recovery or extinction
```

Possible explanations to test rather than assume include:

- the map/UI calls the place rich but its physical usable ecology is not actually rich;
- source materialization, spacing or map-scale caps suppress real terrestrial opportunity;
- resources exist but knowledge/access/activity range/labor prevent use;
- harvest-to-receipt conversion is too weak;
- support/demand accounting turns adequate receipts into chronic deficit;
- hunger classification is overly severe;
- mortality floors or stacked mortality pressures are too strong;
- abundance reduces scarcity but provides too little positive fertility/survival response;
- density/crowding penalties activate too early;
- several individually plausible penalties combine into systematic demographic suppression;
- the observed outcome is empirically defensible once the actual modeled circumstances are known.

Do not select one of these in advance.

### Research basis required

Before tuning, research serious academic evidence from fields including:

- demographic anthropology and human ecology;
- hunter-gatherer and other small-scale population demography;
- historical demography;
- archaeology / archaeodemography / paleodemography where usable;
- fertility, birth spacing and reproductive constraints;
- infant/child/adult mortality and recovery from shocks;
- founder/colonizing populations;
- density dependence, resource surplus/deficit and long-run stationarity;
- plausible growth/decline distributions and doubling/halving times.

Separate supported empirical ranges, contested reconstructions, model abstractions and deliberate
simplifications. One ethnographic case must not become a universal parameter.

### Required regime matrix

Use multiple seeds and starting densities. At minimum compare:

- exceptionally favorable, very low population;
- exceptionally favorable, moderate population;
- exceptionally favorable, high population;
- good/adequate, low and near-equilibrium populations;
- marginal but survivable conditions;
- chronic food deficit;
- severe/repeated shocks;
- recently devastated population in an otherwise favorable habitat;
- isolated small founder group;
- long stable runs without injected catastrophe.

Do not hard-code the expected outcome first. Academic evidence establishes defensible ranges. A
favorable underpopulated world must be capable of meaningful positive growth if the evidence supports
it, but the checkpoint may legitimately conclude that a user's intuitive expectation was wrong.

### Long-run evidence

Run hundreds to thousands of simulated years where practical and report, at minimum:

- annualized growth-rate distributions;
- births and deaths separately;
- doubling/halving times when meaningful;
- cohort/age/dependency structure at the resolution currently represented;
- mortality causes/pressures;
- fraction of time growing, roughly stable and declining;
- hunger and food surplus/deficit exposure;
- crash and recovery behavior;
- density response and resource depletion/crowding;
- causal attribution from ecology/support into demographic outcome.

### Equilibrium rule

Do not add a fixed carrying-capacity value where population magically stops growing. Near-zero growth
must emerge when actual births and deaths approximately balance under the modeled material and social
conditions. A spectacular habitat with 40 people may be far below its eventual equilibrium while a
mediocre habitat with 40 may be near or above it; the model must not force both toward the same hidden
demographic target.

### Boundary with other roadmap items

- Item 10.5 happens only after Items 8, 9 and 10 have produced and integrated the core health,
  provisioning and survival authorities needed for meaningful calibration.
- It may correct measured defects in those earlier authorities only through the explicit reopening
  protocol.
- It does **not** pre-implement Item 17's detailed life-course fertility/pregnancy/childbirth system.
- It does **not** replace WORLD-M0 or Item 11 WORLD-1. If the diagnosis is fundamentally sparse/false
  baseline physical ecology, area scaling or source realization in the sealed WORLD-M0 authority,
  record a concrete WORLD-M0 reopening trigger and correct that authority explicitly instead of
  creating free support, a terrain-food floor or a parallel WORLD-1 ecology fix.
- If the missing fact genuinely requires the later detailed geology/material-occurrence depth owned by
  WORLD-1, record that dependency separately. WORLD-1 is not the owner of baseline M0 living-ecology
  materialization.
- WORLD-1 should not begin until this calibration gate establishes that the connected human survival
  response is understood well enough to distinguish a genuinely excellent environment from a merely
  viable or marginal one.

### Forbidden shortcuts

Do not:

- simply increase fertility or population growth because the user expects growth;
- tune to one 2,000-year trajectory;
- call a place rich solely from Habitat Potential or visual presentation;
- create direct habitat-to-food support;
- lower mortality globally without causal attribution;
- add a carrying-capacity attractor;
- suppress bad outcomes merely to make the simulator feel more optimistic;
- let a later detailed-demography checkpoint excuse a clearly false aggregate survival regime now.

# 6A. Repository-document and static-schema debt register

This register is not another roadmap. It records traps that cut across future items.

## 6A.1 Current-status prose is not self-healing

`CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md` and `docs/HANDOFF.md` preserve valuable reasoning but currently disagree with
local Git. After every freeze, the architect must reconcile their current-status headers or mark them
historical. Appending another status block without retiring the stale headline makes future agents
read mutually exclusive project states.

## 6A.2 Zero-reader and zero-producer names require explicit ownership

Before a future item uses an existing field or enum because the name looks convenient, it must run a
repository-wide writer/reader audit. A zero-reader field can be removed, claimed with a new lived
writer, or retained as compatibility vocabulary—but never silently treated as an old authority.

## 6A.3 Type schemas are not latent production truth

The settlement, diffusion, event, model and chronicle type files are useful design clues. They do not
constrain future implementation unless a frozen invariant explicitly says so. A future model may
replace them after proving no production owner depends on them.

## 6A.4 Projection size is not causal depth

A multi-thousand-line UI/read-model module can be more elaborate than the causal state it describes.
Future audits must trace every public claim backward to state that was produced by lived events. A
projection must not be promoted into a behavior writer merely to avoid building the missing system.

## 6A.5 The Band schema is an accumulation boundary, not an ontology proof

The 133-field `Band` interface currently contains active authorities, cached summaries, read models,
legacy tags, future hooks and dormant vocabulary side by side. Future checkpoints must classify new
state by owner, cadence, invalidation and lifecycle. Adding another optional field is not sufficient
architecture.

# 7. Cross-system risk register for future reviewers

A stronger future model should begin by searching for these recurring failure shapes:

## 7.1 Evocative names without authority

Examples already observed include territorial-pressure fields, status values with no producers,
projection modules whose labels sound behavioral, and simulator history that could be mistaken for
human writing. For every important noun, ask:

```text
Who writes it?
Who reads it?
Does the reader change behavior or only UI?
Can the state resolve, decay or be invalidated?
Does a similarly named second authority exist?
```

## 7.2 Aggregate recomputation that launders people or material

Fixed ratios and convenience recomputation can manufacture cohorts, cure risk, create storage or
erase burden. Any split, merger, absorption, household transfer, task party, settlement aggregation or
polity formation requires before/after ownership ledgers.

## 7.3 Selection mistaken for execution

The Item 2 defect is a reusable warning. A candidate, decision, score, intent, plan, affordance,
problem, idea, institution proposal or migration pressure is not a physical/social attempt. Require an
execution identity, participants, time, material, risk and terminal outcome.

## 7.4 Projection mistaken for mechanism

Identity, knowledge-carrier, material-affordance, camp-foothold, history and story projections are
valuable. They are not behavior unless a production reader consumes a canonical authority. Search
imports and call sites rather than trusting the architecture graph or UI.

## 7.5 One generic status answering several questions

The published code already mixes activity, terminality and legacy markers in some status fields.
Future systems should ask explicit semantic questions such as living, established, provisional,
eligible, terminal, visiting, member, household or polity rather than expanding one enum indefinitely.

## 7.6 Hidden truth disguised as convenience

World tiles, resource stocks, other groups' state, future outcomes and engine event logs are easy for
code to access. Every decision must identify its band/person/household/institution knowledge source and
acquisition path.

## 7.7 Natural zeros and vacuous tests

A branch with zero natural occurrence may be structurally impossible, rare, disconnected or simply
missed by the horizon. Record which. A test that cannot fail or whose non-vacuity predicate is wrong is
not evidence.

## 7.8 Integration by duplicate adapters

When a future item needs richer actors, do not keep an old aggregate authority and add a parallel
richer one indefinitely. Publish migration maps: old invariant, refined representation, retired fields,
adapters and regressions.

## 7.9 Scaling by entity multiplication

The project intends representation to aggregate as social scale grows. Settlements and polities must
not be simulated as ever-growing collections of full-fidelity bands if that breaks the watcher budget
or duplicates people.
