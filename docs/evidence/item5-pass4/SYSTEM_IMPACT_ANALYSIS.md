## Past systems consumed

Pass 4 consumes the following existing authorities and evidence channels without replacing their ownership:

1. `practicalFragments` — existing bounded technical primitives and their lived/inherited evidence.
2. `inventionChain` — existing Item-5 problem/idea/experiment chain, migrated from catalog-exclusive hypotheses to bounded compositional hypotheses while retaining historical compatibility.
3. `practicalResponses` — existing response/effect boundary and Pass-2 execution-provenance gate. Existing physical coefficients remain reachable only through their already-supported effect adapters and execution classes.
4. `resourceKnowledge` — band-known resource/patch memories and observation provenance used only as evidence from which coarse human material beliefs may be formed.
5. Existing environment observation channels — observed tiles, place/context memories, trip/scout/work outcomes, fire/camp observations, and other already-legitimate band-known evidence. These channels may support weak material encounters; they do not grant competence.
6. Crossings / temporary watercraft — actual crossing outcomes and practiced temporary-watercraft evidence remain legitimate technical-fragment evidence. Pass 4 does not create new crossing execution authority.
7. `waterWorks` — the existing narrow physical-work authority remains authoritative and is not generalized into construction, inventory, or crafting.
8. Item-4 inheritance/fission — the accepted daughter-transfer seam remains the owner of fission. Pass 4 only extends Item-5 daughter adaptation transfer with degraded epistemic hints; it does not alter Item-4 fission mechanics.

## Past systems requiring migration

1. Terrain/material shortcuts inside practical fragment generation.
   - Direct wooded-context derivation of sealing/frame material competence must be removed from `practicalFragments` and routed through a human-material-evidence seam.
   - Membrane/shaft/generic-stone material claims must be audited and separated from legitimate technique evidence.
   - Actual route, crossing, staging, temporary-watercraft, waterworks, and other physically observed technique outcomes remain valid evidence.
2. Current catalog-exclusive candidate generation.
   - Existing historical `variantKey` values remain stable.
   - The existing variant catalog becomes bootstrap templates, recognizable labels/compatibility aliases, and physical-effect adapters; it is no longer the universe of possible designs.
   - Candidate generation becomes fixed-budget composition over human-known evidence only.
3. Blanket fragment failure weakening.
   - Current response/experiment failure handling can weaken every supporting fragment together.
   - Pass 4 replaces this with typed feedback and localized attribution. Unknown causality remains unknown and may reduce design confidence without fabricating component blame.
4. Item-5 read models/projections.
   - Existing Item-5 projections must expose design signatures, material beliefs, source provenance, experiment execution proof, typed results, revision lineage, dormancy, convergence, and present local reproducibility without creating a second state machine.
   - `adaptiveHuman` and legacy technology surfaces remain compatibility/read models only.

## Past systems proven unaffected by scope

The following systems are outside Pass-4 ownership and must remain behaviorally/authoritatively unchanged unless implementation inspection proves a concrete compatibility edit is necessary. Any such exception must be called out explicitly in Pass-4 evidence rather than folded into this claim.

1. Item-3 social authority.
   - Pass 4 does not make `socialEcologicalDiffusion.ts` canonical.
   - Live technical copying is allowed only if an already-canonical bounded Item-3 transmission event/evidence seam is found; otherwise copied provenance remains schema-capable but live diffusion is deferred.
2. Item-4 fission mechanics.
   - Pass 4 does not change founder allocation, departure, travel, stabilization, reintegration, fission timing, or any Item-4 physical lifecycle.
3. Demography.
   - No fertility, mortality, cohort, population, viability, or demographic-pressure formula belongs to Pass 4.
4. Food stock resolution.
   - No plant/fauna/aquatic stock, physical harvest, food receipt, support, or depletion formula belongs to Pass 4.
5. Mobility execution.
   - Pass 4 may learn from already-recorded route/crossing/trip outcomes but does not add residential or party movement authority.
6. Environmental physical truth.
   - Terrain, biome, hydrology, material occurrence, geology, stock, weather, passability, and other world-truth authorities are not redefined by Item 5.
   - Candidate generation is forbidden from querying raw terrain/world truth directly.

## Future systems depending on this Pass-4 contract

1. WORLD-M0 — physical material occurrences/properties must feed the material-evidence provider without replacing human epistemic state.
2. WORLD-1 — later world-detail layers must preserve the same observation-to-belief boundary.
3. Task/labor — must later replace estimated experiment labor/opportunity with real task-level execution accounting.
4. Production/craft — must later own acquisition, transformation, quantities, components, outputs, and actual craft execution.
5. Construction — must later own durable built works beyond the narrow existing `waterWorks` authority.
6. Teaching — must later replace aggregate inherited/copied hints with individual or otherwise canonical skill-carrier transmission where that authority exists.
7. Culture — must later own causal adoption, prestige, taboo, convention, and social persistence rather than Item 5 manufacturing them.
8. Trade — must later own physical transfer of materials/artifacts and any associated access proof.
9. Metallurgy — must later deepen material/process predicates and execution authority rather than appear as an Item-5 technology-tree branch.

## Temporary abstractions in Pass 4

1. Coarse physical material evidence provider.
   - It adapts already-legitimate local observations into weak encounter/handling/property evidence.
   - It does not classify geology, scan the world, grant materials, or grant technical competence.
2. Band-aggregate material beliefs.
   - `HumanMaterialBelief` is canonical human technical knowledge at the band aggregate resolution.
   - It is not physical material truth, inventory, ownership, an object, or an individual skill carrier.
3. Physical effect adapters only for existing supported responses.
   - Existing named variants retain their already-supported response/effect adapters and execution provenance classes.
   - A novel compositional design with no physical executor/effect adapter may exist as knowledge/idea/plan/revision but receives zero invented physical coefficient and zero fabricated efficacy.

## Named future replacements / migrations

The temporary abstractions above have explicit replacement seams rather than permanent hidden assumptions:

1. `WORLD-M0 material occurrence/property authority` replaces the coarse physical-material evidence provider beneath the existing observation-to-human-belief seam. Human beliefs remain canonical epistemic state and may be wrong, stale, inherited, or context-limited.
2. `canonical inventory / production / craft authority` replaces all current plan-only material-role bindings when real acquisition, quantities, components, objects, transformation, consumption, ownership, and artifact outputs are implemented.
3. `task/labor authority` replaces estimated experiment labor/opportunity cost with actual scheduled/expended work and physical execution proof.
4. `individual teacher / skill-carrier authority` deepens aggregate inherited/copied technical hints into causal transmission among represented carriers where the social model can support it.
5. `causal culture/adoption authority` replaces projection-only diffusion/adoption language with legitimate social persistence, rejection, convention, prestige, taboo, and adoption causality.
6. `physical trade authority` replaces copied/material-access implications with actual movement and receipt of materials/artifacts between groups.
7. `durable construction authority` replaces the current narrow physical-effect-adapter limitation for persistent structures; `waterWorks` remains a historical narrow authority until migrated deliberately.
8. `metallurgy authority` introduces specialized thermal/material transformation, material classes, production constraints, and resulting physical artifacts only after material/execution authorities exist.
9. `dynamic weathering and local material-compatibility authority` deepens current coarse durability/weathering and local-reproducibility predicates with physical environmental interaction.
10. `future physical-response execution adapters` are mandatory whenever a new Item-5 response attempts a physical effect; knowledge or intention alone may never become execution authority.
11. `foundational world-substrate migration seam` allows future geology/hydrology/material-locality changes to replace the evidence provider while keeping Item-5 human beliefs, provenance, contradiction, and revision semantics intact.

## Scope invariant for implementation

The Pass-4 compositional candidate generator may consume only human-known Item-5 state and explicit band-known evidence adapters: practical problems/history, technical fragments, human material beliefs, inherited design hints, typed prior feedback/revisions, and context/evidence references already observed by the band. It must have no direct raw-terrain, biome, geology, hidden-world, WORLD-M0, inventory, stock, or full-map query.

This impact record is intentionally conservative. Later Pass-4 evidence must either confirm these unaffected-system claims with source/test audits or name any concrete compatibility exception; it may not silently broaden scope.
