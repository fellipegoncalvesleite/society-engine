# Roadmap Item 5 Pass 4 — Future Evolution Contract

Status: final closure-correction future-evolution contract pending independent architect review. It defines what the corrected Pass-4 tree owns now and which future authorities require deliberate reopening/migration. It does not accept or freeze Item 5.

## Complete at current Item-5 resolution

At the current band-aggregate resolution, `band.practicalAdaptation` is the single canonical causal/history authority for:

- perceived practical problems;
- bounded technical fragments;
- band-level human material beliefs;
- bounded primitive construction inputs;
- normalized compositional design hypotheses;
- optional recognition against historical designs after composition;
- deterministic fixed-budget candidate selection;
- selected ideas and experiment plans;
- `underway` versus `blocked_by_execution` lifecycle truth;
- existing execution-provenance integration;
- typed observed-result interpretation;
- localized material/operation/component feedback;
- revision/dead-end/dormancy memory;
- practical responses and efficacy history;
- degraded inheritance of problem framing, fragments, material beliefs, and design hints;
- read-only diagnostic projection/explainability.

Historical named variants remain useful recognition/compatibility/effect-adapter records. They do not define the discovery universe.

## Permanent epistemic and physical separations

These distinctions must survive every later migration:

```text
physical world/material truth != human material belief
historical belief != currently actionable belief
```

and:

```text
observation
!= hypothesis
!= plan
!= execution
!= result
!= efficacy
!= competence
!= artifact
```

Knowledge may contain observations, hypotheses, and retained history, but none of those stages may be collapsed into execution authority or physical result.

A later physical system may prove that a material exists, can be extracted, is possessed, or was transformed. None of those facts may be backfilled merely from human belief or a design label.

## Primitive-composition rule

Complete technology categories/templates must not define the invention universe.

Future expansion of invention must preserve:

```text
bounded reusable primitives + human-known evidence + constraints/history
→ composition
→ normalized design identity
→ optional historical recognition
```

It must not regress to:

```text
complete catalog entry
→ candidate
```

Historical catalogs may remain useful for recognition, migration, compatibility, and already-authorized physical adapters.

## Material-memory/actionability rule

Historical material memory may persist after **current actionability** decays.

Future systems must preserve separate questions:

1. what evidence/history the band retains;
2. how strong that evidence is for a current material-property role here and now.

Staleness must not require arbitrary deletion of provenance. Conversely, stored confidence may not act forever as current proof. Re-observation may reinforce/reactivate the same belief identity when the evidence truly supports it. Inherited belief is not automatically fresh local evidence.

## Blocked-plan lifecycle seam

`blocked_by_execution` means a hypothesis/plan exists but current execution authority does not.

Future execution systems may provide a truthful transition out of this state only when they prove the missing physical prerequisites. Until then:

- attempt count stays zero;
- execution proof stays absent;
- physical result stays absent;
- efficacy does not mature.

A future task/craft/construction authority must integrate with the existing canonical experiment/history lifecycle rather than create a second competing plan authority.

Final long-run certification exposed and corrected immediate post-eviction cycling of unchanged blocked plans. Recent selected `composed:` ideas now provide a bounded 32-tick reconsideration cooldown after their blocked experiment falls out of the cap-4 store. The same plan may be reconsidered after that interval; the current resolution has no authority for permanent impossibility. Future task/material authorities may replace this coarse cooldown with evidence-sensitive retry semantics, but must not turn it into an unbounded global blacklist.

## Localized failure rule

Future richer materials and tasks must preserve attribution locality. Precise material/role/property blame requires precise evidence. Ambiguous failure cannot globally poison a material or fabricate a property diagnosis. Later fresh evidence may supersede a bounded local lesson where architecture permits.

## Mandatory reopening triggers

Item 5 must be deliberately reopened/migrated when any of the following becomes authoritative:

1. WORLD-M0 or equivalent foundational world-material authority;
2. canonical geology / material occurrence / material properties;
3. physical extraction;
4. inventory / material stock / possession;
5. craft / transformation authority;
6. production systems;
7. task-level experiment/craft labor;
8. individual teaching / individual skill carriers;
9. social or cultural adoption/transmission;
10. physical trade / transfer of materials or artifacts;
11. construction;
12. metallurgy;
13. richer material/weather/climate compatibility;
14. any new physical execution authority for a practical response or experiment.

These triggers are reopening seams, not work authorized by the current checkpoint.

## Required migration behavior by future authority

### WORLD-M0 / geology / material truth

May replace or deepen the temporary physical observation substrate. Must remain upstream of human observation/belief. Human belief may be incomplete, stale, inherited, or wrong even when canonical material truth is exact.

### Extraction / inventory / craft / production

Must own real quantities, possession, transformation, consumption, components, outputs, and persistent artifacts. Item-5 design/material belief may constrain or request work but cannot manufacture those quantities.

### Task-level labor

May replace coarse plan labor/risk/opportunity estimates with performed tasks. It must distinguish planned labor from actual work and connect execution evidence back to the canonical experiment lifecycle.

### Individual teaching / skill carriers

May replace aggregate degraded inheritance with causal person/carrier transmission. It must not reinterpret current band-level belief as individual mastery.

### Social/cultural adoption

May create causal diffusion/adoption authority. It must not promote the current read-only projection or legacy compatibility state into a writer by convenience.

### Physical trade

Must transfer real stock/artifacts through a physical authority. Sharing a design hint or belief is not physical trade.

### Construction / metallurgy

Must own actual transformation, material consumption, labor, outputs, persistence, maintenance, and failure. A named design/history entry cannot stand in for those processes.

### Richer weather/material compatibility

May deepen which physical executions are feasible. It may inform observations and execution constraints but must not overwrite human material belief directly from hidden truth.

## Intentional current-resolution limitations

The current closure-corrected Item-5 model remains coarse in these explicit ways:

- band-aggregate rather than person-level knowledge/skill;
- coarse material categories/property predicates rather than canonical geology;
- temporary existing-observation-to-material-belief adapter;
- plan-level labor/risk/opportunity rather than task accounting;
- narrow existing effect adapters rather than general craft/production;
- degraded aggregate inheritance rather than teacher/carrier transmission;
- no causal social/cultural adoption system;
- coarse local reproducibility rather than full weathering/material interaction;
- blocked composed plans use a bounded 32-tick reconsideration cooldown; periodic retries after that interval remain a coarse current-resolution seam.

None is silently declared solved by this checkpoint.
