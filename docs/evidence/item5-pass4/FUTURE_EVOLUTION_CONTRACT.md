# Roadmap Item 5 Pass 4 — Future Evolution Contract

Status: closure-candidate contract. It defines what is complete at current Item-5 resolution and exactly which future authorities require deliberate reopening/migration.

## Complete at current Item-5 resolution

At the current band-aggregate resolution, Item 5 now has one canonical causal/history authority for:

- perceived practical problems;
- bounded technical fragments;
- band-level human material beliefs;
- normalized compositional design hypotheses;
- deterministic fixed-budget candidate generation;
- selected ideas and experiment plans;
- existing execution-provenance integration;
- typed observed-result interpretation;
- localized feedback attribution;
- revision/dead-end/dormancy memory;
- practical responses and efficacy history;
- degraded inheritance of problems/fragments/material beliefs/design hints;
- read-only diagnostic projection/explainability.

Historical named variants remain useful bootstrap/compatibility/effect-adapter entries without defining the whole invention universe. Raw environment truth does not act as a technology tree. Need does not create capability. Knowledge does not create objects. Design does not prove execution.

## Intentional coarse seams

The following abstractions are intentionally coarse rather than missing silently:

- band-aggregate rather than person-level knowledge/skill;
- coarse material categories/property predicates rather than canonical geology;
- temporary existing-observation-to-material-belief adapter;
- plan-level labor/risk/opportunity estimates rather than task accounting;
- existing narrow physical-effect adapters rather than general craft/production;
- degraded aggregate inheritance rather than teacher/carrier transmission;
- projection-only social diffusion where no canonical transmission seam exists;
- coarse local reproducibility from human-known contexts rather than full weathering/material interaction.

## Mandatory reopening triggers

Item 5 must be deliberately reopened/migrated when any of the following becomes authoritative:

1. WORLD-M0 canonical material occurrence/property truth;
2. canonical extraction/inventory/craft production;
3. task-level experiment labor;
4. individual teachers / skill carriers;
5. causal culture/adoption;
6. physical trade;
7. durable construction;
8. metallurgy;
9. richer climate/weathering/material compatibility;
10. any future practical response attempting a physical effect without an execution authority;
11. foundational physical-world changes to material locality/accessibility.

For each reopening, preserve the epistemic boundary: physical truth may deepen what can be observed, but `HumanMaterialBelief` remains what the humans know/believe and may remain uncertain, wrong, stale, inherited, or context-limited.

## Permanent contract

**ROADMAP COMPLETION DOES NOT MEAN PERMANENT IMMUTABILITY.**

Future authorities may deepen and migrate accepted Item-5 abstractions through named seams. They must not silently bypass or duplicate the accepted causal authority merely because Item 5 was previously completed.

A later physical-world/material substrate may replace the temporary evidence provider underneath the human observation boundary. A later inventory/craft authority may replace plan-only material roles with real acquisition, transformation, consumption, components, ownership, and outputs. A later task authority may replace estimated labor with performed labor. A later culture/teaching/trade system may replace aggregate hints/projections with causal transmission.

## Item 6 / world-generation handoff

This contract does not implement Item 6, WORLD-M0, or a roadmap rewrite. `docs/HANDOFF_ITEM6_WORLD_GENERATION.md` remains the explicit handoff. Before Item-6 implementation, the next architect must discuss the world-generation proposal with the user rather than silently accepting, rejecting, or resolving it inside Item 5.
