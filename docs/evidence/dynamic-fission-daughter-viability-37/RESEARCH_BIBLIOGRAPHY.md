# Roadmap Item 4 — research bibliography

Companion to `RESEARCH_CONSTRAINTS.md`. Each entry names the **implementation constraint it
supports** and **how it is used**: direct evidence, synthesis, analogy, or disputed interpretation.

**No entry is turned into a formula.** No camp size, founder size, sex ratio or fission threshold is
taken from any of them. They decide *which quantities are allowed to matter* and *which distinctions
must exist*.

---

## How this bibliography is and is not used

**Used for:** deciding that separation must be a reversible process; that several independent causal
routes must exist; that viability is about composition and material capability; that failure and
reintegration are normal outcomes; and that knowledge bounds destination.

**Not used for:** any numeric threshold, any group size, any founder ratio, any establishment
horizon, or any coefficient. Every number in the implementation is an **implementation abstraction**
chosen for boundedness and named as one — never sourced from a paper.

**Disputed material is cited as disputed.** Dunbar (1992) appears only because it is explicitly
excluded, with Lindenfors et al. (2021) as the reason.
