# CORRECTION-16 — Research and anthropological constraints

This checkpoint made **no coefficient change and no production behaviour change**, so it
introduced no new empirical calibration. This document records (a) the constraints that
remain binding on the retracted claims, and (b) the constraints that will bind the unbuilt
scope, so a later checkpoint does not have to rediscover them.

---

## 1. Constraints that apply to the retracted social claim

CORRECTION-15 concluded the social layer was inert and inferred from that a design direction:
"real cooperation needs the relationship/household substrate". The empirical inference was
sound in general terms but the measurement supporting it was invalid, so it must not be
treated as an established constraint.

What the ethnographic and behavioural-ecology literature does support, and what remains
binding:

- **Social organisation in mobile foragers is causally load-bearing, not decorative.** Group
  fissioning, tolerated scrounging, food transfers, and residential flexibility are widely
  documented responses to variance in return rates. A model in which social state changes
  nothing would be the anomalous case, not the default. The CORRECTION-16 measurement is
  consistent with the literature; the CORRECTION-15 measurement was not.
- **Cooperation must move something physical.** The prohibition CORRECTION-15 recorded — no
  generic cohesion bonus, no survival scalar — survives its retraction and is independently
  justified. Sharing, care labour, party participation, route information and coordinated camp
  work are distinct mechanisms with distinct costs; collapsing them into one multiplier is the
  §3.9 "detached content pack" failure.
- **No universal script (§3.8, §16.2).** Fission thresholds, tolerance norms, and sharing
  rules vary; they must arise from local pressure and history, not from a fixed constant
  applied to every band.

## 2. Constraints on the death-memory mechanism

The mechanism re-verified here — bereavement producing bounded, decaying fertility suppression
tied to *realized deaths only* — matches the constraint adopted at FOOD–DEMOGRAPHY-SEPARATION-2
and is unchanged:

- energetic and psychosocial stress may suppress fertility *before* severe deprivation
  produces a large mortality hazard;
- recovery should release the temporary suppression;
- the suppression must be driven by actual losses, not by a food *label* copied into severity.

CORRECTION-16 confirmed the first and third points locally and did **not** measure the second
(§6.3 decay/recovery was not built). Society-specific birth intervals and life expectancies
are still deliberately not copied.

## 3. Constraints that will bind the unbuilt scope

Recorded now so the next checkpoint inherits them.

### Frontier exploration (§11 — not built)

- **Central-place foraging and logistical mobility** bound how far a party can go and return:
  provisioning, water, carrying capacity, and a return budget are the real limits, not a
  radius constant.
- **Landscape learning is directional and incremental.** Foragers extend known country along
  corridors, cues, and remembered bearings — not by sampling the globally richest unseen
  cell. Any target selection that uses world truth is disqualified regardless of how it is
  bounded.
- **Second-hand information is real but degraded.** Reports carry direction and existence more
  reliably than quantity, and trust is relationship-dependent. Returning parties should update
  approximate rather than exact knowledge.
- Information must remain party-local until physical return. Knowing a place exists is not
  knowing its resources, and neither is competence to exploit them.

### Cohort and labour causality (§8 — not built)

- Dependency ratios and the age profile of working adults are documented constraints on
  foraging return in small-scale societies, but the effect is mediated — through who can
  travel, how far, and how long — not through a direct per-capita penalty.
- The **magnitude** claim is exactly what CORRECTION-16 downgraded to `UNRESOLVED`. Do not
  assume the effect is small (CORRECTION-15's 0.01) or large; measure it with paired-seed arms
  and a full mediation waterfall.

### Adaptation cascade (§10 — not built)

- Innovation in foraging societies is overwhelmingly incremental and problem-driven, arising
  from lived difficulty rather than from a research process, and it frequently fails or is
  abandoned.
- Adaptation may improve recovery probability. It must **not** guarantee survival, and a
  successful response must never be inserted directly to demonstrate the mechanism.

## 4. Method constraint established by this checkpoint

The most important constraint CORRECTION-16 adds is methodological rather than
anthropological, and it is recorded in full in `AUDIT_ADMISSIBILITY.md`:

> A negative result is only evidence if the instrument could have detected a positive one.

CORRECTION-15 produced three confident negative results — social inertness, cohort
insignificance, and a regression classification — from instruments that were structurally
incapable of returning the opposite answer. Two are now disproven and one is downgraded. Any
future claim that a subsystem "does nothing" must first demonstrate that the measurement would
have registered it if it did.
