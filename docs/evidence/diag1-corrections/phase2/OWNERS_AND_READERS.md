# Owned semantics and reader inventory

The generated `NUTRITION_READER_INVENTORY.txt` and `PROVISIONAL_UNIT_INVENTORY.txt` record the actual source references. They are navigation evidence, not substitute test results.

| Owner | Phase2 contract |
| --- | --- |
| humanFoodSupport | One conversion of usable raw harvest into support at100; physical take/loss/depletion/request fields stay raw. Resident interval closure subtracts its bounded receipt cursor. |
| provisionalTravelSubsistence | Actual completed daily span `[D-1,D)`, support/demand/stress/water measures, exclusive open interval and actual-day close. Operation and site evidence use converted support. |
| nutritionExposure | Validate half-open intervals, exact revision, prefix extension, overlap refusal, unknown gaps, clipping, duration and demand weighting. Keep720 completed days plus one bounded open interval. Compensated aggregation avoids record-partition rounding artifacts. |
| seasonalSurvival | Sole persisted nutrition history and its projections. Current condition is the last completed day; recent is360days; chronic is720days; season-valued streaks are physical days/90. Pending residential demand measures actual bodies per day. |
| nutritionMigration | Pure atomic version transition at world advancement: known legacy ordinary90day measurements and complete retained provisional chronology only. Unsupported historical social-unit contexts refuse without modifying input. |
| demography | Annual reader asks for the previous360completed physical days at the real simulation date. Its accepted de-stacked response coefficients remain unchanged. |
| pressure / movementFatigue | Latest four genuine displacements contribute0.2 each, decaying linearly over7days. Daily replacement preserves unrelated pressure fields and the uncapped nonmovement component; initial pressure absence stays absent. |
| departure / reintegration | Validate the real permit before outgoing closure; apportion/recompose dated embodied experience over the same lived days without food duplication or additional elapsed time. |

Behavioral nutrition readers use the owned duration projections: pressure/mobility, demography, viability, acute risk, fission residual/preparation, body/camp logistics, biome/dry-margin/practice/camp affordances and social context. Their thresholds remain unchanged. Identity/events/history/visible-nature readers use the same current-condition projections. `bandChronicle` scans retained records for descriptive history; it does not own annual or behavioral time aggregation. Broader current-living projection semantics remain a deferred DIAG-1 finding.

Provisional return, independent-operation, stabilization, establishment and post-return social decision readers now receive support in the same units. Daily `usableUnits` remains raw; the exact physical source discriminators and migration controls verify both sides of this distinction.
