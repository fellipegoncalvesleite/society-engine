Integer day boundaries and overlap rejection bound completed record and segment counts; no deposit-ID set or duplicate food stock is introduced. Residential pending demand and the receipt cursor are bounded scalars.

The focused adversarial daily history reaches721 retained records and551568 serialized bytes; it remains bounded after repeated extension. This is deliberately the large one-day-record representation, not an ordinary residence. Movement history keeps its existing80-entry bound; the observed final3 maximum is80. Physical source/depletion ownership remains unchanged.

No new cache is introduced in Phase2. Phase1B world/time/object scopes remain weak; a seasonal crossing has at most8 capability combinations per live input tuple. The plant memo holds one current generation per live tile. These caches are not serialized or indexed by an ever-growing year table.

All runs start with34 people and retain bounded population in these controls. Durations include concurrent audit load and are telemetry, not an isolated causal performance ratio or a scalability guarantee. The candidate does more physical trips in most arms; no population, habitat or source coefficient was selected for runtime. Source hashes and run configurations are stored with every natural payload.

The runtime and state envelope does not certify arbitrarily many bands, dense interaction graphs, empirical demographic validity or the unrecovered2000-year case. Future health, life-course, work-group and world-provider replacements require explicit migration and renewed boundedness checks.
